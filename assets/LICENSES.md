# Animation asset credits

The supplied animation files are sourced from LottieFiles and published under the [Lottie Simple License](https://lottiefiles.com/page/license). Attribution is encouraged. Each asset's author and original page are recorded in `assets-manifest.json`.

The `scripts/fetch-assets.mjs` installer downloads original `.lottie` assets into `public/assets/lottie/` at build/install time. Binary assets are **not** included in this Git repository. Never claim that the repo itself stores them.

No paid or premium animations were selected. Do not resell or redistribute the assets as a standalone collection.
